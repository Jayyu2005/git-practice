Article: https://krasimirtsonev.com/blog/article/the-naked-truth-about-ai-assisted-coding
I found this article interesting because it gives a realistic view of AI coding tools instead of just hyping them up or dismissing them completely. The author has years of real experience as an engineer, so he talks about actual problems he has run into, not just theory. What stood out to me most was the part about security and licensing risks, since that connects directly to what I want to do in cybersecurity. It made me think about how fast people are adopting AI tools without fully thinking about what gets exposed or copied in the process.
